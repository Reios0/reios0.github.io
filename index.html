That range means I gravitate toward the messier problems: the half-finished spec, the platform that doesn't behave the way the docs say it should, the script that fails silently at 2am. I'm comfortable working with designers, clients, and non-technical stakeholders to turn a rough idea into something real, and I pick up new tools quickly when the project calls for it.
